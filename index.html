Frequently Asked Questions

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes, if you are comfortable entering data in a spreadsheet. The Quick Start Guide and User Guide cover setup, and the Sample Workbook shows how the system looks when filled in. Basic Excel familiarity helps.

Do I need Microsoft Excel?

The tracker is designed for Microsoft Excel. Minimum supported Excel version: [TO CONFIRM: add minimum version]. Notion is needed only for the Notion workspace.

Does it work with Google Sheets?

It is designed for Excel. Google Sheets compatibility is not guaranteed, and some features may not work there.

Are stock prices updated automatically?

No. There are no live market feeds or automatic price updates. You update prices manually, and calculations depend on the data you enter.

Does it support Indian mutual funds?

Yes. You can record mutual fund holdings and transactions, including regular investments, and analyse them with XIRR. Values are entered by you.

Can I track fixed deposits and gold?

Yes. Fixed deposits, bonds, gold and other asset types can be recorded alongside stocks, mutual funds and ETFs.

Does the Notion workspace synchronize with Excel?

No. Excel and Notion are independent workspaces and do not synchronize automatically. Data must be entered or transferred manually.

Is this an investment advisory service?

Can I use it across multiple devices?

You can open the files on any device that supports Excel and Notion. Excel files are not synced between devices unless you store them in a cloud service of your choice.
